## What to check after the print

1. Did Q2 EPS continue the beat streak, or break it?
2. Revenue against the ~$31.3M consensus (+43.5% YoY).
3. Realised metal prices vs volumes — is growth coming from output or just
   from silver prices? Only the first is durable.
4. H2 guidance, which management had pointed toward being stronger.
5. Where the stock sits relative to the $11.99 high and $5.15 July low.
6. Whether the R² to silver has risen (more commodity proxy) or fallen (more
   company story).
